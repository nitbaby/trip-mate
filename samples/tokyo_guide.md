# Tokyo Quick Guide

## Getting around
- Buy a Suica or Pasmo IC card at the airport and use it on trains, subways and buses.
- Haneda Airport to Shinjuku takes about 35 minutes by Keikyu line and JR.
- Most trains stop running between midnight and 5:00 AM.

## Food
- Vegetarian options are limited. Look for shojin ryori (Buddhist temple cuisine) and say "niku nashi" (no meat).
- Many broths contain fish stock (dashi), so ask when ordering.
- Convenience stores (Lawson, 7-Eleven) sell onigiri with ume or kombu fillings.
- Tipping is not customary.

## Money
- Cash is still common. 7-Eleven ATMs accept foreign cards.
- Tax-free shopping is available for tourists with a passport at many stores.

## Useful numbers
- Police 110. Ambulance and fire 119.
