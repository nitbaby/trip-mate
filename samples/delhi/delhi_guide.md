# Delhi Quick Guide

## Getting around
- The Airport Express metro line links Delhi airport Terminal 3 to New Delhi station in about 20 minutes; tickets cost under 100 INR.
- Prepaid taxis are booked at the counter inside the arrivals hall. Ride-hailing apps also work. Always agree the fare first for an auto-rickshaw.
- The metro is the fastest way across the city; avoid the evening rush from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM.

## Food
- Vegetarian food is everywhere; many restaurants are fully vegetarian. Ask for "Jain" food if you avoid onion and garlic.
- Drink only bottled or filtered water, and avoid ice in street food stalls.
- Tipping: 10 percent is usual in restaurants when no service charge is added.

## Sights and timings
- The Taj Mahal in Agra is closed on Fridays.
- The Red Fort is closed on Mondays. Humayun's Tomb is open daily from sunrise to sunset.
- Dress modestly at temples and mosques, cover shoulders and knees, and be ready to remove shoes.
- February mornings are foggy and cool, about 8 C, rising to about 22 C in the afternoon.

## Money
- Cards are widely accepted in hotels and restaurants; carry small notes for taxis and markets.
- Official money changers at the airport give fair rates; avoid street changers.

## Useful numbers
- Police, fire and ambulance (all-India emergency number): 112.
- Tourist helpline: 1363.
