INDIA ENTRY RULES: E-VISA, MEDICATION AND CUSTOMS (summary for travelers, fictional demo text)

Visa
Most visitors need an approved e-Visa before boarding. Apply online at least 4 days before arrival and carry the approval (printed or on your phone).
The passport must be valid for at least 6 months on arrival and have two blank pages.
Always check the official government site before travel: rules and fees change.

Bringing medication into India
Prescription medicine should stay in its original packaging with a copy of the prescription and a doctor's letter in English.
Medicines containing narcotic or psychotropic substances (for example some strong painkillers and sleeping tablets) need a doctor's prescription and may be limited to a personal supply of about 30 days.

Customs
Foreign currency above 5,000 USD in notes, or above 10,000 USD in total, must be declared on arrival.
Indian rupees may not be brought in or taken out above 25,000 INR per person.
Duty-free allowance for adults: 2 litres of alcohol and 100 cigarettes.
Drones may not be brought in without prior permission.
