# Seattle Quick Guide

## Getting around
- Link light rail runs from Seattle-Tacoma Airport to Westlake station downtown in about 40 minutes. Pay with an ORCA card or a contactless card.
- Ferries to Bainbridge Island leave from the downtown terminal about every hour; the crossing takes 35 minutes.
- Rain is common in January: carry a light waterproof jacket rather than an umbrella, as locals do. Daytime temperatures are about 4 to 9 C.

## Food
- Pike Place Market has many vegetarian and vegan options; most restaurants mark vegan dishes on the menu.
- Seafood is the local specialty; ask about gluten-free options, which are widely available.
- Tipping is customary: 18 to 20 percent in restaurants. Sales tax (about 10 percent) is added at the register.
- Coffee shops are everywhere and many close by 6:00 PM.

## Money
- Cards are accepted almost everywhere; carry little cash.

## Useful numbers
- Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 911.
- Washington Poison Help: 1-800-222-1222.
