US ENTRY RULES: CUSTOMS AND MEDICATION (summary for visitors, fictional demo text)

Documents
Visitors need a valid passport. Canadian citizens generally do not need a visa for tourism; most other nationalities need an approved ESTA or a visa before boarding. Always check the official government site before travel.

Bringing medication into the United States
Prescription medicine should stay in its original container with the pharmacy label, ideally with a copy of the prescription or a doctor's letter.
Bring only a personal supply, normally no more than 90 days.
Cannabis and products containing it remain illegal under federal law, even though they are legal in Washington State. Do not carry them across the border or on a flight.

Customs
Cash or monetary instruments worth 10,000 USD or more must be declared on arrival.
Duty-free allowance for adults 21 and over: 1 litre of alcohol and 200 cigarettes.
Fresh fruit, vegetables, meat and plants are not allowed without declaration and inspection.
