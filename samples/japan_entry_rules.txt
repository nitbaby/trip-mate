JAPAN ENTRY RULES: MEDICATION AND CUSTOMS (summary for travelers)

Bringing medication into Japan
Most prescription medicines may be brought in for personal use in quantities up to a one-month supply.
Carry them in the original packaging together with a copy of the prescription and a doctor's letter in English.

Medicines that contain stimulants, such as some ADHD medications and pseudoephedrine in cold remedies, are prohibited in Japan.
Strong painkillers and narcotics, including codeine in higher doses, require a Yunyu Kakunin-sho import certificate in advance.
For more than a one-month supply, or for restricted medicines, apply for the import certificate from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare before travel.

Over-the-counter medicine is limited to a two-month supply. Vitamins are limited to a two-month supply.

Customs
Visitors may bring in 400 cigarettes and 3 bottles of alcohol (760 ml each) duty-free.
Cash or securities worth more than 1,000,000 JPY must be declared at customs.
Fresh fruit, meat and meat products are not allowed without inspection.

Always confirm current rules with the Japanese embassy or the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, as rules change.
